Welcome to KAFUOSA

We Care For Sight,
One Outreach At A Time.

KAFUOSA is the official optometry students association at Kaimosi Friends University. We bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world community eye care through outreach, mentorship, and professional development.

Our mission is to cultivate the next generation of optometrists deeply committed to serving underserved communities across Kenya.
