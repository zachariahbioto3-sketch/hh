About KAFUOSA

The Kaimosi Friends University Optometry Students Association (KAFUOSA) is the official body representing optometry students at Kaimosi Friends University. We are committed to fostering academic excellence, professional development, and community outreach in eye care across Kenya.

Academic Excellence

Supporting students through resources, study groups, and peer mentorship.

Community Outreach

Running free eye screening camps and donating glasses across underserved communities.

Professional Network

Connecting students with alumni, mentors, and industry professionals.
