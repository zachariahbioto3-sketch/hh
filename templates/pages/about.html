About KAFUOSA

The Kaimosi Friends University Optometry Students Association — committed to academic excellence, community eye health, and professional development across Kenya.

Our Story

Advancing Vision Science
In Kenya

KAFUOSA was established by optometry students at Kaimosi Friends University with a shared commitment to solidarity, professional excellence, and service to the community. We operate under the authority of the university's departmental regulations and the Republic of Kenya.

From free eye screening camps in rural communities to academic seminars and mentorship programs, KAFUOSA bridges the gap between education and real-world impact in vision care.
