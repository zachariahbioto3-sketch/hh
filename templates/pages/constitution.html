KAIMOSI FRIENDS UNIVERSITY OPTOMETRY STUDENTS' ASSOCIATION (KAFUOSA)

CONSTITUTION

PREAMBLE.

We the students of optometry and vision sciences appreciate the need and strength brought about by unity and we are hereby pleased to establish the association of all students in the department of Optometry and vision sciences. Our commitment to solidarity and institutionalization of this association is of utmost consideration. We commit ourselves to be a self-determined association within the authority of the departmental regulations, Kaimosi Friends University Act and statutes and the Republic of Kenya. We therefore declare that this constitution shall guarantee continued peaceful existence of the association of students known as Kaimosi Friends University Optometry student's Association (KAFUOSA). As the students in the department of Optometry and vision science, we dedicate and bind ourselves to this association and hereby adopt this constitution in its entirety and the all-encompassing document that will represent a legal contract between the students and KAFUOSA. This document shall be respected and appropriately acknowledged by all members at all times as the guiding document of the Kaimosi Friends University Optometry student's Association (KAFUOSA).

ARTICLE 1 A; NAME.

The name of this organization shall be the Kaimosi Friends University Optometry student's Association (KAFUOSA), which is established under Kaimosi Friends University Students Organization.

ARTICLE 1 B; AREA OF OPERATION.

This constitution shall be applicable to all the members of optometry and vision science in Kaimosi Friends University.

ARTICLE 2 A; MOTTO.

The KAFUOSA motto shall be Eyes To The Future.

ARTICLE 2 B; VISION.

To offer the best vision and visual environment to humanity through optometry and vision science.

ARTICLE 2 C; MISSION.

To fulfill the vision and eye care needs of the public through clinical care, research and education of all which enhance the quality of life.

ARTICLE 3. AIMS AND OBJECTIVES.

The Association shall be an official representative of the students of Optometry to both the School of Health Sciences and The Kaimosi Friends University Administration:

a) Serving as liaison between the students, department, faculty and staff.

b) Promoting harmony among students, faculty and staff.

c) Participating in the yearly welcoming of incoming First Year students in the department of Optometry.

d) Promoting such other functions as the Association shall deem necessary and within its province.

e) Non-Discrimination Policy, as dictated by the Kaimosi Friends University: This organization and its members shall not discriminate against any individual(s) for reason of race, color, creed, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, sex, age or handicap.

ARTICLE 4; MEMBERSHIP.

Members shall register with Ksh 200 and pay a yearly subscription of Ksh 100. Both the registration and subscription fees should be forwarded to the treasurer within two (2) weeks after general elections, which are non-refundable. All activities of the Association that involve students shall be carried out exclusively by the registered members.

ARTICLE 4 A; MEMBERSHIP CATEGORIES.

The Association shall consist of: a) Patron, b) Honorary members, c) Life members, d) Registered members, e) Associate members.

PATRON.

Is invited by the Executive Council to be a Patron of KAFUOSA. The Patron is entitled to all privileges of KAFUOSA except to hold offices and to vote. The term limit of the Patron shall be 1 year, renewable based on performance and member satisfaction.

HONORARY MEMBERS.

Individuals who have contributed or rendered outstanding service to KAFUOSA. They are conferred the title "Honorary member" by the General Meeting on the recommendation of the Executive Council. They shall pay Ksh 1,000 non-refundable registration fee payable once.

LIFE MEMBERS.

Practicing Optometrists entitled to all privileges. They shall be eligible to hold office but ineligible to vote. Life membership is open to practicing optometrists who have been active members for two consecutive years and shall pay Ksh 1,500 registration fees.

REGISTERED MEMBERS.

a) Students must be enrolled at Kaimosi Friends University pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Optometry and Vision Science. b) Registered members shall have all privileges of membership. c) Membership shall be renewable annually via a yearly subscription fee of Ksh 100. d) Registered members shall have the right to hold any executive council office and can be class representatives.

ASSOCIATE MEMBERS.

Department of Optometry staff, ophthalmologists, and health professionals in related fields may become associate members. They are entitled to all privileges except holding executive office and voting.

ARTICLE 4 B; CESSATION OF MEMBERSHIP.

Membership shall cease upon: a) Written resignation submitted to the Secretary. b) Failure to pay annual subscription within the stipulated period. c) Expulsion by the Executive Council for conduct unbecoming of a member. d) Graduation or departure from the University (for student members).

ARTICLE 5; EXECUTIVE COUNCIL.

The Executive Council shall consist of: Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary General, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Assistant Treasurer, Academic Secretary, Outreach Coordinator, Welfare Officer, and Public Relations Officer.

ARTICLE 6; ELECTIONS.

Elections shall be held annually by secret ballot. All registered members in good standing are eligible to vote. Candidates must be registered members who have paid their dues. The Electoral Commission shall be appointed by the outgoing Executive Council.

ARTICLE 14; AMENDMENTS.

Amendments to this constitution may be proposed by any registered member in writing to the Secretary General. Proposed amendments must be circulated to all members at least 14 days before a General Meeting. A two-thirds majority vote of members present at the General Meeting is required for adoption.

ARTICLE 15; DISSOLUTION.

KAFUOSA may be dissolved by a resolution passed by not less than three-quarters of registered members at a Special General Meeting called for that purpose. Upon dissolution, all assets shall be transferred to the Department of Optometry and Vision Science, Kaimosi Friends University.

ARTICLE 16; CONSTITUTION EFFECT.

This constitution shall come into force upon adoption by the founding General Meeting and shall supersede all previous rules and regulations of KAFUOSA.
