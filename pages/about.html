About KAFUOSA

The Kaimosi Friends University Optometry Students Association (KAFUOSA) is the official body representing optometry students at Kaimosi Friends University. We are committed to fostering academic excellence, professional development, and community outreach in eye care across Kenya.

Academic Excellence

Supporting students through resources, clinical study groups, and peer mentorship programs.

Community Outreach

Running free vision screening camps and donating prescription glasses across underserved communities in Kenya.

Professional Network

Connecting vision science students with alumni, clinical mentors, and global optometry professionals.
